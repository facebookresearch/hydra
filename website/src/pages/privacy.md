# Privacy policy

_Last updated: August 12, 2026_

This policy explains how the Hydra project handles information when you visit
the Hydra website at [hydra.cc](https://hydra.cc/).

## Information collected

The website does not require an account and does not ask you to provide
personal information directly.

Like most websites, our hosting and network providers may process routine
technical information needed to deliver, secure, and maintain the site. This
may include your IP address, browser and device type, requested pages, the date
and time of a request, and the referring page.

### Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand, in aggregate, how the website and its
documentation are used. Its default measurements include page and session
activity, approximate location, and browser and device information. Google
Analytics may set first-party cookies, including `_ga`, to distinguish users
and sessions.

Google Analytics uses IP addresses during collection to derive approximate
location information. Google states that IP addresses are discarded before
the data is logged in Google Analytics. We do not use Google Analytics to
identify individual visitors or for advertising, and we do not intentionally
send names, email addresses, or other directly identifying information to it.

Google processes Analytics data under its own terms and privacy practices. For
more information, see
[Google's explanation of how it uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Other service providers and external sites

The website is delivered using Cloudflare and GitHub Pages, and its search
feature is provided by Algolia. These providers may process technical request
or search information under their own privacy policies. The website also links
to external sites. Their privacy practices apply when you visit them.

## Your choices

You can block or delete cookies using your browser settings. Google also offers
a [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
Blocking cookies or analytics may not prevent processing that is necessary to
deliver or secure the website.

## Data retention

Analytics event data is retained according to the retention settings of the
Hydra Google Analytics property. Aggregated reports that do not identify
individual visitors may be retained for longer. Other providers retain
information according to their own policies.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website or its services change. We will
update the date at the top of this page when we do.
